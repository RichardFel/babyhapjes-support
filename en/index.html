Help with Papilu

Papilu guides you through your baby's first foods, day by day. Here are answers to common questions. Can't find yours? Just send us an email.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your own phone: there is no account and nothing is sent to us. If you delete the app, your data is gone. A backup of your phone may carry it over to a new device, but that is not guaranteed.

I bought Premium, but the schedules are still locked.

Tap a locked schedule and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. You won't be charged again.

How do I change the start date or start age?

Go to Schedules and tap your baby's card. There you can change the name, date of birth, start age and start date.

How do I change the language, feeding guideline or units?

Go to Schedules and tap the gear at the top right (Settings). Under Feeding guidance you choose which country's advice to follow: the Netherlands, the United States, Spain or Brazil.

Can I add more children?

Yes. On Schedules, tap Add a child. Each child has their own plan, and Premium covers all your children.

How do I start a schedule over?

Open the schedule from Schedules, tap the menu at the top right and choose Clear progress. This can't be undone.

My baby reacted to a food.

Open the day from Schedules and add a reaction flag with a short note. Papilu gives general guidance and is not medical advice. If in doubt, contact your baby's doctor, and call your local emergency number (911 in the US) if your baby has trouble breathing or a swollen face.
