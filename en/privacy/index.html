Privacy policy

How Papilu handles your data: everything stays on your own phone.

Last updated: November 2026

1. What data do we store?

Papilu stores all data exclusively on your device, using your operating system's local app storage. We do not collect, send or sell any personal data to external servers.

  • Baby's name (optional)
  • Date of birth and start date (optional)
  • Daily progress: ticks, taste rating, notes, reaction flag
  • Your own recipes and tips (optional)

All of this stays on your device and is never shared.

2. No tracking or analytics

Papilu uses no analytics services, advertising networks or tracking cookies. No usage statistics are sent to us.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (Premium) go through the App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment details; those are managed entirely by Apple or Google. See their privacy policies for more information.

4. Deleting your data

You can clear all stored data via a schedule's menu → Clear progress, or by deleting the app from your device.

5. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email hapsiesupport@gmail.com
